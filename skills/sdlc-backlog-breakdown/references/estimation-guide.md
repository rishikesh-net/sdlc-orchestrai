# Estimation guide
Use relative points: 1 (trivial, < half day), 2 (about 1 day), 3 (1 to 2 days), 5 (2 to 3 days, consider splitting),
8 (too big: split). Estimate the whole slice (migration, API, UI, tests), not one layer.
Spikes: set a timebox in hours or days and the question to answer; leave estimate null.
Epics are not estimated; they are the sum of their children.
When two items feel equal but one has unknowns, size up the one with unknowns or add a spike.
